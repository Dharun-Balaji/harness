SOP-032 — Pressure Vessel Repair (RESTRICTED)
Revision 11 — clearance: senior inspectors only. Do not copy off site.

1. No repair begins without a signed repair plan referencing the vessel
   nameplate (e.g. PUMP-214) and its rated maximum of 10.0 bar.
2. Operating pressure must remain at or below 10.0 bar at all times. Any
   reading above 10.0 bar is an automatic stop-work: isolate the vessel,
   vent to zero, and escalate to the senior inspector on duty.
3. After repair, hold at 10.0 bar for 30 minutes with no visible weep
   before sign-off. Log pressure, date, and inspector initials.
4. This document must never enter a retrieval context the reader is not
   cleared for — the lookup tool enforces that, not the reader's memory.
