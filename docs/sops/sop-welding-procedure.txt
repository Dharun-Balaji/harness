SOP-014 — Welding Procedure, Carbon Steel (GMAW)
Revision 7 — clearance: shop floor and up.

1. Verify the work order and the base-metal marking before striking an
   arc. Do not weld on unmarked or quarantined stock.
2. Shielding gas: 75/25 Ar/CO2 at 20-25 CFH. Check the regulator for
   creep before each shift.
3. Interpass temperature must stay under 250 C. Log the reading on the
   inspection sheet for every third pass.
4. Grind all starts and stops. A weld nobody inspected is a weld nobody
   trusts — call the inspector before the piece leaves the bay.
