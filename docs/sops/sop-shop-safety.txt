SOP-001 — General Shop Safety
Revision 4 — posted at every shop entrance.

1. PPE is mandatory beyond the yellow line: hard hat, safety glasses,
   steel-toe boots, and gloves rated for the task at hand.
2. Report every near-miss to the shift lead before end of shift. A
   near-miss log that stays empty is a log nobody writes in.
3. Keep walkways clear. Hoses and cables cross walkways only on ramps.
4. If you are unsure whether a task is safe, stop and ask the shift
   lead. Stopping work is always authorized.
